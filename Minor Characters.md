# Helvanthor

## Old Tobin

**Role:** Bread seller in the Helvanthor lower market. Pays Dusty in bread to guard his stall.
**Appears:** Scene 1. Remembered in Scenes 8 and 10.

### Physical Description

Past sixty and bent from forty years over a kneading board. His beard has gone the gray of ash, and he trims it short with a bread knife to keep it out of the dough, though flour and chaff still salt it. Thin gray hair lies plastered under a flat wool cap gone shiny with grease. Small dark eyes sit deep in a face creased like an old crust, the nose broad and red-veined from cookfire smoke. His hands are the broadest thing about him, floury to the wrist, knuckles swollen thick, both wrists marked with pale crescents from oven doors. A canvas apron stained brown and gray hangs to his shins, and he clutches it over his mouth when sickness comes near. He smells of rye, woodsmoke, and wet canvas.

### Personality

A market survivor who keeps his head low and his loaves turned crust-out to the street. He grumbles at the rain, at the smell of his guard, at the price of rye, and he pays fair anyway. He hired a boy who stinks of the Greys because that boy keeps the thieves off his bread, and he has never asked where Dusty sleeps. He stumbles back from the sick and goes still before armored strangers, because he knows which of them can ruin him faster. Under the grumbling he keeps a quiet ledger of decency: an extra loaf for ugly work, a hand that holds the bread a breath longer than it needs to. He would never walk into the Greys for Dusty. He would never cheat him either.

### Dialogue

**1. Gruff disgust**
*Scene 1. Dusty's stink drifts over the loaves.*
> "Gods, boy." … "You'll turn my dough before it rises."

**2. Awed unease**
*Scene 1. Valia and her two Mythrandis guards walk into the market. His hands go still on the bread.*
> "Mythrandis." … "What's the academy want in a place like this?"

**3. Grim gratitude**
*Scene 1. The noon bell, after Dusty burns the sick man's hand. He pushes the loaves into Dusty's arms and doesn't let go right away.*
> "Three today." … "For the sick one. That was ugly work."

# The Guard

## Holt

**Role:** Mythrandis Guard. Valia's escort from Helvanthor to Aethres.
**Appears:** Scenes 2–7. Dismissed at the Silver Swan.

### Physical Description

Big through the chest and shoulders, twice Maedelyn's width in steel. Polished plate fits close across his chest, and his helmet leaves a slit for the eyes and a gap for a heavy, square jaw that works beneath the steel whenever he's handed an order he doesn't like. A great blue shield marked with the white tower rides his back, a heavy spear rides his shoulder, and his metal boots ring off the cobbles. With the helmet tucked under his arm he shows a man near forty: close-cropped brown hair mashed flat with sweat, small pale eyes, a wide nose webbed with broken veins, a neck burned red by years of road. He eats like a man who expects to march at dawn, and the cheese wheel always goes missing on his side of the table.

### Personality

A veteran who leads with the spear and thinks second. He sorts the world by threat, and a hooded boy in the Greys with blood on his knuckles sorts one way. Valia's chatter rolls off him, but he knows her better than she knows herself: when she digs through her pockets for the papers, he knows they sit in the inside left one. He shares Pell's contempt for Dusty with a look and a huff through his nose and lets Pell do the talking. Before the Maids he crumbles. He won't turn his helmet toward Maedelyn's back, and when he's dismissed his voice cracks and he bows to the Maids instead of the lady he served. Steady, dutiful, short on imagination, and a coward in exactly one direction.

### Dialogue

**1. Alarm**
*Scene 2. He levels his spear down the lane at Dusty after the fight in the Greys.*
> "Halt!"

**2. Patient duty**
*Scene 3. Valia empties pocket after pocket at the Way Station doors, hunting for the papers.*
> "Left side, my lady," … "Inside."

**3. Fear**
*Scene 7. Dismissed at the Silver Swan, he bows low to the Maids, not to Valia.*
> "Th-thank you, my lady." … "Maids. It was an honor. A great honor."

## Pell

**Role:** Mythrandis Guard. Valia's escort and Holt's junior partner.
**Appears:** Scenes 2–7. Dismissed at the Silver Swan.

### Physical Description

Fifteen years younger than Holt and leaner, long in the arm, his plate cinched tight over a narrow frame. His thin mouth curls back from his teeth in the Greys and presses flat when he's ordered off. Out of the helmet he has a narrow face, a sharp nose, hard pale eyes set close together, and thin dishwater hair that spills across his forehead when he bows. His color betrays him. Red floods his face in a heartbeat and drains just as fast, until he matches the pale road dust on his armor. After Maedelyn turns him back on the road, his hands tremble on the reins for a mile.

### Personality

A snob in borrowed steel. He comes from a step or two above the gutter and guards that step with his whole chest; "street filth" comes easy to him because the gutter sits closer than he likes. He snorts at a sick girl's wonder and at an honest question, curls his lip at a boy for wanting his sister to eat, and swings his spear down the moment Dusty answers back. He backs down faster than he charges. When Dusty names what the city guard does to the Greys, Pell looks at his boots, so a conscience lies buried in him somewhere, under the contempt for the poor and the terror of the Maids.

### Dialogue

*Pell speaks only twice on the page.*

**1. Contempt**
*Scene 2. Dusty won't give Valia his name.*
> "The lady asked you a question, street filth. You'll give her an answer, and you'll give it with respect—"

**2. Cowed**
*Scene 7. Dismissed at the Silver Swan, two days after Maedelyn turned him back on the road. He echoes Holt, faint.*
> "An honor."

# The Maid Corps

## Gwenith

**Role:** Maid Corps. One of the two Maids who look after Valia's lab, and now Rinnie's own Maid in Aethres.
**Appears:** Scenes 4–9.

### Physical Description

Short, the top of her head barely clearing Dusty's shoulder, and soft-built: the apron ties cinch a narrow waist between full hips and a fuller chest that pulls the black bodice taut across its buttons. Light brown hair swept into a high ponytail falls to the small of her back and whips around when she turns. Her hazel eyes catch the light in flecks of green and gold. Rose is painted on her cheeks and lips, and her dimples cut deep when she smiles, which is often. She has a round, sweet face with a small upturned nose and a scatter of faint freckles beneath the paint. She wears the Corps uniform: a black dress buttoned snug to the throat, long sleeves ending in black lace gloves, a crisp white apron, a skirt that stops just above the knee, white stockings, and black stiletto heels that make no sound on cobbles, on flagstones, or on the groaning third stair of the Silver Swan. Mending lives in her apron pocket. She hums while she works.

### Personality

Warmth with a blade folded inside it. She gushes, coos, applauds every empty plate, and calls Rinnie "little sparrow" and "sweet pea" within days of meeting her. She wanted to keep the siblings within minutes and pushed Maedelyn until she asked permission. Ask her about the Corps and she turns slippery, answering in riddles with a glance toward Maedelyn. She cries easily, and she knows Maedelyn will look at her when she does. When Holt and Pell lower their spears on the road, the dimples vanish, and she stands in the swaying cart with her hands folded and doesn't blink. She is a Maid first. Her love comes out as tasks: brushing hair, counting bites, ordering the soft bread instead of the crusty kind, finding and furnishing a whole house in two days. At the bridge she holds up Rinnie's ribbon and says nothing at all.

### Dialogue

**1. Delight**
*Scene 4. Dusty and Rinnie bow their thanks in the Duke's town, Rinnie from inside his arms.*
> "Oh, look at them. Look at them, Maedelyn. They bowed. Both of them. She bowed from inside his arms." … "Can we keep them? Please say we can keep them."

**2. Guarded**
*Scene 5. Brushing Rinnie's hair in the women's room while Rinnie asks how to become a maid.*
> "Well." … "Some are asked. Some ask."
>
> "This and that. Cleaning. Cooking. Tea." … "Very important tea."

**3. Pleading**
*Scene 7. She catches Maedelyn's sleeve at the Silver Swan, eyes flicking to Dusty and back.*
> "Maedelyn." … "You have to talk to them. About the permission. You promised." … "You promised."

## Maedelyn

**Role:** Maid Corps. Gwenith's senior partner in Valia's lab. Manages Valia, the road, and everyone on it.
**Appears:** Scenes 4–8. Arranged Dusty's barber, uniform, and trunk for Scene 9.

### Physical Description

A head taller than Gwenith, willowy and straight-backed; she stands beside a door as straight as a candle. Her hips run slim beneath the apron, and her chest sits heavy against the black bodice. Dark blue hair falls straight and glossy past her shoulders without a strand astray, even after five days on the road. At night she draws the pins, lays them in a neat silver row on the washstand, and the hair slides down to the small of her back. Eyes of the same deep blue, lined sharp in kohl, take in a whole market in one unhurried sweep. She has a long, pale face with high cheekbones and a narrow chin, and a mouth whose corner tugs toward a smile and gets pressed flat before it can finish. She wears the same Corps uniform as Gwenith on heels thin as needles, and she rides a black mare with her gloved hands loose on the reins.

### Personality

The Corps' discipline given a body. She's clipped, exact, and three steps ahead of everyone: she watched the party leave the Way Station, made "certain assumptions" and had a covered pony cart waiting, with side panels that open so Rinnie could look out. She refuses chairs and speaks in complete, formal sentences. Her exasperation shows as closed eyes and a long breath through the nose. She manages Valia like a gifted younger sister, defends her when Valia can't defend herself, and keeps her honest about favorites with one raised brow. When guards threaten a student, she crosses the road faster than an eye can follow and speaks cold as well water. When Valia shakes, she goes soft as polished wood. She guards the Corps' secrets, yet she told a twelve-year-old about the monster, more than Valia had heard from any Maid in forty years. Dusty's "I'm one of the grateful ones" almost makes her smile. She doesn't let it.

### Dialogue

**1. Cold warning**
*Scene 6. Holt and Pell lower their spears on Dusty. She's across the road before anyone sees her move.*
> "Guardsmen." … "Your duty is the protection of the academy's students. It is not their correction." … "I would remind you that the protection of students is the duty of the Maid Corps as well. From any threat."

**2. Gentle comfort**
*Scene 6. Afterward, her gloved hand laid over Valia's white knuckles.*
> "It is finished, Lady Valia. No one will raise a hand against you or your guests." … "You are quite safe."

**3. Exasperation**
*Scene 4. Right after Gwenith begs to keep the siblings. Eyes closed, a long breath out through her nose.*
> "Lady Valia. Guardsmen. Young man. Young lady." … "This way, please. Supper is waiting, and the rooms will not remain warm on their own."

## Maid Oriel

**Role:** Maid Corps. Led Dusty's intake group of thirty on his first day.
**Appears:** Scene 9.

### Physical Description

Tall for a woman, though she still tips her chin a long way up to meet Dusty's eyes. A sweep of copper-red hair is pinned high off a long neck, with a few loose curls left at the nape. Heavy kohl frames gray eyes the color of rain-dark slate, and her brows are plucked to fine arches. Her lips are painted deep red, and they don't move while her eyes travel from a student's boots to his hair and back. She has a strong, elegant face with a straight nose. Beneath the black bodice she's full-figured, a narrow waist above generous hips, the white apron sitting on her as if it were cut for no one else. She wears the Corps uniform, her heels silent on stone. Her voice carries low and even to every corner of a courtyard without strain.

### Personality

The Corps' face to every new class: precise, unshakable, total authority without volume. One look silences a snickering boy until his ears go pink. Four words answer a girl in gold who expects better rooms. She recites the daily schedule like scripture and forbids anyone to ask who decided it. She has led more first years through that courtyard than any of them have lived years, and nothing they do surprises her. She watches everything, and at the last door her gray eyes rest on Dusty a breath longer than on the others. She saw something in him, and she keeps it to herself.

### Dialogue

*Oriel's voice never changes. The third state shows only in her eyes.*

**1. Command**
*Scene 9. The white courtyard of lime trees, opening her speech to thirty first years.*
> "Welcome to Mythrandis." … "I am Maid Oriel. For the remainder of today, you are in my care. Tomorrow you are in your own."
>
> "Curfew is ten o'clock, and it is not a suggestion."

**2. Icy dismissal**
*Scene 9. A girl in gold asks what happens if her room is unsuitable.*
> "It will not be."

**3. Quiet interest**
*Scene 9. The last stop at the northern dormitories. Her eyes stay on Dusty longer than they did on the others.*
> "Up the stairs. Fourth floor. The seventh door on your left."

*Continuity: the Summary flags Oriel's "All actions must be with Beauty, Elegance, and Dignity above all else." for cutting, since the creed belongs to the Maids alone. It's left out of the samples.*

## Maid Sorrel

**Role:** Maid Corps. Serves the hidden swan courtyard where Auriel takes tea.
**Appears:** Scenes 12 and 15.

### Physical Description

Small, a head shorter than Auriel and slight as a reed, her black shoulders narrow enough for Dusty's shadow to swallow whole. Copper-red hair is twisted into a knot so tight it draws at the corners of her eyes, and those eyes are a pale, clear blue, the color of lake ice. She has a fine-boned, pointed face, porcelain pale, with a dusting of freckles across the nose that her powder leaves showing, and thin lips painted soft coral. Her body is compact and slender, with a dancer's tiny waist, small and high beneath the black bodice, her legs long for her height in their white stockings. She wears the Corps uniform. Her small gloved hands carry a full silver tray of pot, cups, cozy, and cake tin without a tremor. Her face stays serene, too serene, calm as the black water that pools over the buried river in the Greys.

### Personality

She shows two faces, and Dusty can't fit them together. With Auriel she's patient and almost fond. She holds firm on the tea rule, then hands Auriel tomorrow like a gift wrapped in lace, and when Auriel frets over the angle of a teapot handle, Sorrel tells her plainly that the tea is perfect. With Dusty she needles and tests, and her small smile carries a cruel corner. She has cleared away a great many second cups in that courtyard, and she says to his face that she's waiting to see when he'll stop coming. She coughed to stop Auriel saying too much. She claims Maids are neutral, then reminds him they have eyes and ears. Any question she won't answer gets her back. Whose side she's on is still open.

### Dialogue

**1. Gentle**
*Scene 12. Clearing the tea, her gloved fingers paused over the cakes, her pale eyes lifted to Auriel.*
> "Tomorrow is a fresh day, Miss Landsdon," … "Tea will be served at four, as always. And perhaps this fine young gentleman could join you here again. Should he find his way a little faster."

**2. Needling doubt**
*Scene 12. Alone with Dusty, laying the cakes one by one in a tin, not looking up.*
> "It's good to see someone talking to Miss Landsdon." … "I do wonder," … "how long you'll last."
>
> "You'll learn," … "Like the rest of her so-called friends."

**3. Veiled threat**
*Scene 12. Dusty steps in close and asks her to be more specific. Then he reminds her that Maids are supposed to be neutral.*
> "You should pick your battles more carefully, young man," … "Trying to intimidate a Maid on your first day is not a very good idea."
>
> "We are." … "But we have eyes. And ears."

# Faculty

## Professor Brannoch

**Role:** Introduction to Magic.
**Appears:** Scenes 10 and 13.

### Physical Description

Taller than Dusty and half again as broad, so big he turns his shoulders to fit through a door. A russet beard is cut square across his barrel chest, russet hair cropped short above a ruddy, weathered face, and his nose was broken once and set by someone in a hurry. White teeth flash through the beard when he grins, which is often. He shoves his sleeves past his elbows over forearms thick as Dusty's thighs, and his fingers are the size of sausages; a stick of chalk in his fist looks no bigger than a grain of rice, and it snaps on the last stroke of every word. The lectern groans when he leans on it. His voice rattles the windows in their frames.

### Personality

A bellowing, generous showman who loves teaching and loves watching first years jump. Big jokes, big gestures; he flings his arms wide enough to span half the stage to show an ocean. He picks up each class exactly where the last one stopped, as if the night never happened. Under the noise sits a serious teacher. When it matters he drops his voice so low the back rows lean forward to catch it, and what matters most to him is that nobody empties themselves showing off. He ribs his colleagues, and by Auriel's account he holds martial dearest: hear anyone call healing the best and he'd cry, then hand out push-ups.

### Dialogue

**1. Exuberant**
*Scene 10. The door cracks against the wall on the first morning, and the windows rattle.*
> "GOOD MORNING!"
>
> "I am Professor Brannoch, and this is Introduction to Magic, and if any of you are in the wrong room, now is the time to slink out with your dignity." … "Splendid! Then let us begin with the only thing that matters."

**2. Playful**
*Scene 10. On students who lean two ways, two thick fingers raised.*
> "A rare few of you will lean two ways at once." … "Blended. We'll fight over you in the faculty lounge."

**3. Grave**
*Scene 10. On managing mana, his voice dropped so low the back rows lean in.*
> "So you manage it. Every class, every duel, every spell. The student who empties himself showing off at noon is the student who can't lift a candle at six. Learn how much you have, and learn how much you've spent. It matters more than anything else I'll teach you this year."

## Professor Venn

**Role:** Magical Application. Runs the elemental pen at alignment testing.
**Appears:** Scenes 10 and 13.

### Physical Description

An elf, slender and straight-backed, her silver hair cropped close to her skull so nothing softens the lines of her face: high, sharp cheekbones, a long straight nose, a narrow chin, pointed ears bare of any ornament. Her eyes are a pale silver-gray, and they move across two hundred faces without hurrying. She has lived for centuries and looks forty at most. Severe charcoal-gray robes fall straight from her shoulders and gather at a slim waist, and she stands with her hands folded behind her back, weight even, as unmoving as the stone benches around her. Her hands give off no glow when she works. Her voice is soft, and it still reaches the top bench as clearly as if she stood beside you.

### Personality

Stillness as authority. She waits for a hall to hush, then waits a little longer. She speaks little, and every sentence lands where she aims it. She values the work over pedigree; arriving ahead of the others is "no great virtue, only an accident of tutors and family." Entitlement gets one line and then her back. Beginners get patience. She has watched five generations of noble children cross that bridge certain of themselves. Dusty fears her eyes more than anyone's on the island, because they would see the black haze and the silver.

### Dialogue

**1. Serene**
*Scene 10. Guiding two hundred students, eyes closed, to find their mana.*
> "Now find the place just beneath your breastbone. Do not reach for it. Let your attention settle there, the way dust settles in still water. There is warmth there. For some of you it will feel like a coal. For others, like sun on stone. It has always been there. You have simply never been told to look."

**2. Cutting**
*Scene 10. The boy in the crimson blazer asks to skip a test his family's tutors already gave him.*
> "Some of you are ahead," … "Others are not. All of you will be tested, and all of you will be placed into your alignment classes afterward, according to what the test shows. Not according to what your tutors told your mother." … "Dismissed."

**3. Austere**
*Scene 13. Sorting two hundred students into three roped pens.*
> "Professor Stroud will see to martial," … "Professor Tantris will see to healing. I will see to elemental. Go where you believe you belong. If you are wrong, you will be told."

## Professor Mottram

**Role:** Potions: Foundations (mixed years).
**Appears:** Scenes 10 and 14.

### Physical Description

A thin gray woman past sixty, all wire and knuckle, with bony wrists and a long face that years over braziers have scored and reddened. Her spectacles live pushed up into a frizz of iron-gray hair, and she peers over their rims more often than through them. Her eyebrows are singed thin, her forearms freckled with old burn scars, and her fingertips stained a permanent yellow-brown that no soap will lift. A leather apron scorched black along its hem hangs from her neck, dusted white with chalk. She writes in a narrow, slanting hand at speed and erases with her sleeve. She smells of green things and burning, the same as her room.

### Personality

Dry, clipped, and impatient with fools and charlatans. She offers no introduction. A pot lid clanged against the table starts her class and ends it. She makes jokes and never smiles at them. She's exacting enough to make a whole room repeat "Coughwort" back to her. Under the vinegar sits a deep humility about her craft: she believes the best methods burned with the monasteries and lie buried with the herbwives, that some of them beat her own, and that a student might someday know one more than she does. Auriel sums her up: "She's wonderful. She's terrifying."

### Dialogue

**1. Clinical**
*Scene 10. Holding a glowing blue flask up to the window light.*
> "This is a mana restorative," … "It is not a miracle. Drink it empty and it will fill perhaps a third of your reserve, and you will have a headache for the rest of the afternoon, and if you drink three in a day you will spend the night vomiting."

**2. Sardonic**
*Scene 10. On the rule that every potion does one thing.*
> "Anyone who wants a potion that cures everything may leave now and go become a charlatan in Aethres. You will make a great deal more money."

**3. Earnest**
*Scene 14. On the preparations lost to fire, war, and time.*
> "And just as many were lost. Burned with the monasteries that kept them. Buried with the herbwives who never learned their letters. Forgotten in the wars, and there have been a great many wars. So understand me. There are many ways to prepare every plant on this table, and some of them are better than mine, and nobody alive knows them. I will teach you the ways I know. Learn them well, and when you are my age, perhaps you will know one more."

## Professor Stroud

**Role:** Martial discipline. Runs the martial pen at alignment testing.
**Appears:** Scene 13.

### Physical Description

Human, past fifty, squat and square as a mounting block, with a bull neck and hands like split oak. His iron-gray hair is shorn to stubble. His nose was broken flat and set crooked, and the tip of his left ear is gone, the old wound puckered pink. Dark eyes sit deep beneath a heavy brow, and his flat mouth never moves, whether he's barking or pleased. A leather jerkin scuffed pale with buckle marks hangs over plain linen, with a rag tucked in his belt for wiping down weapons. Behind him stands his rack: wooden swords, blunted spears, quarterstaves, and a pair of iron-headed maces, every one turned so the grain runs the same way.

### Personality

A soldier who came back from the north and treats weapons as comrades. His sense of order runs down to the grain of every staff on his rack, and he corrects fifty grips a quarter-inch at a time without tiring of it. He has few words and no humor, and a boy who leans on a spear gets the full crack of his voice. He took the bull-necked noble into martial before Tantris sent the boy to Venn.

### Dialogue

*Stroud speaks only twice on the page.*

**1. Sharp anger**
*Scene 13. A boy at the rope leans on the butt of a spear.*
> "Take your hand off that."

**2. Grave reverence**
*Scene 13. Wiping the shaft clean where the boy's palm touched it.*
> "You do not lean on a spear, lad. A spear is not a walking stick." … "In the north, a spear is the last friend you have in the world. Treat it like one."

## Professor Tantris

**Role:** Healing discipline. Runs the healing pen at alignment testing. Tested Dusty and named him a healer.
**Appears:** Scene 13.

### Physical Description

An old elf, the first Dusty has ever seen. Elves hold their youth for centuries, so his years run past counting. His long brown hair is tied loosely at the nape, with gray running through it in broad streaks from the temples. Fine lines crease the corners of his clear blue eyes and bracket his mouth, and they deepen when he smiles. He's lean and pale, with long healer's fingers. His robe is undyed wool, cream as fresh curds, the sleeves rolled to the elbow. He stands loose, his weight settled easy in the sand. Up his sleeve he keeps a small knife, the blade no longer than his thumb, for nicking his own finger.

### Personality

Patient and kind, and the joke is always on himself. He teaches healing as a request, never a command. Students who fail go off to the other pens with a kind word and a pat on the wrist, and he beams at talent as though a student had handed him a gift. Cross him, the way the bull-necked noble did by lying to himself for his father's sake, and his voice drops low and even and cuts to the bone without once rising. He saw Dusty's black and silver, called it lovely ("a color I haven't seen in a few years"), and bent to the next student without a second look. His name echoes Valia's father, Tantrus.

### Dialogue

**1. Gentle humor**
*Scene 13. He nicks his own finger in front of thirty nervous students.*
> "Nothing to fear. It's my finger, not yours." … "Find your warmth, the way Professor Venn showed you. Bring it out to your hands. Then lay it over the cut and ask it to close. Don't order it. Ask. Who's first?"

**2. Quiet rebuke**
*Scene 13. The bull-necked noble tells him Stroud has already placed him in martial.*
> "Young man. You have as much potential for a martial alignment as I have for waking up one morning a human." … "Those were built with iron bars and a great many breakfasts, I'd wager, so your father would see a swordsman across the dinner table. It won't change a thing. None of us chooses where we lean. Off you go to Professor Venn, quickly, and let her test you properly."

**3. Delight**
*Scene 13. Dusty's black glow with its silver rim closes the cut in seconds.*
> "Healer," … "Not a doubt in my mind. Go on over with the others, son." … "That's a color I haven't seen in a few years. Lovely."

# Mentioned, Not Seen

People who are spoken of or remembered but never appear on the page.

- **Tantrus Velanthus.** Valia's father, never named on the page. He's there as the old brown robe folded at the foot of her bed at the Gilded Pheasant, elbows patched and hem worn thin, and she rubs its patch when Rinnie asks about her friends. He's behind her freeze at "Ever since—". Per Valia's sheet, he was a professor who died sealing a summoned demonic god in the catacombs at her graduation. His name echoes Professor Tantris; whether that's deliberate is still open.
- **Dusty and Rinnie's parents.** Unnamed. Their father mended boots at the table by candlelight and sang off-key. Their mother kept the flour in the corner where the roof dripped, and Rinnie now wears the face her mother made when Dusty tracked mud across the floor. The family lived in two rooms above a cooper's shop in the lower city until the rent rose and the Greys took them. Eight years ago the city guard burned their street with the parents inside. Dusty has told only Valia.
- **Rariel.** Not on the page yet; her dinner scene was cut and isn't canon. Per Auriel's sheet, she was taken from the same village as Auriel at age two and became the Church's model Saintess. She was once Auriel's closest friend, and she's now at the academy on Church orders, quietly steering students away from her.
- **The Duke.** Unnamed. He rules the honey-brick town where the Way Station set the party down, from a pale stone manor on a rise flying a blue banner. Seen only from across town.
- **The ones who said yes.** Whoever the Maids answer to. Maedelyn carried Gwenith's request to them, and they granted it. They have no name and no face yet.
