# Continuity Log

Discrepancies between the Manuscript Summary, the Manuscript, and the reference files (Lore Bible, the character sheets, Minor Characters.md). Checked through Scene 15 (the second tea).

Line numbers point into `Manuscript` (M) and `Manuscript Summary` (S). Severity:

- **High** — touches a core mechanic or a main character's arc; needs your call before more pages build on it.
- **Medium** — a visible fact contradicts a sheet; one side needs updating.
- **Low** — detail drift, a polish item, or something the page only implies.

Nothing in the Manuscript or the Summary was changed. This file only logs.

## Part 1 — Manuscript Summary vs. the reference files

### 1A. New discrepancies (not in the Summary's Continuity Flags)

**1. Dusty's mana is a literal fire. His sheet doesn't give him fire.** — High
- Summary: "a candle flame, orange, with a black haze and a hairline silver edge" (S189). He burns a man's hand with it (S16) and blinds a boy (S23).
- Dusty sheet: "Like a flame on a candle — small, but it never runs out. Black glow with a silver outline." The candle is a metaphor for his supply, and the glow is black. Discipline: Healing only.
- Lore Bible and Brannoch (M1114) both say fire is elemental. Putting fire in his hands plus a healing test makes him **Blended** (healing + elemental), which his sheet doesn't say. The Summary logs it as an open "ASK VALIA" question (S116) but never flags that it conflicts with the sheet.
- Call: is Dusty blended? If so, update his sheet. If not, the flame needs to read as healing mana, not elemental fire.

**2. How rare is black and silver?** — High
- Summary: Tantris says "That's a color I haven't seen in a few years" (S156). The open thread asks "Who else had that color a few years ago?" (S262).
- Dusty sheet: "a rarity not seen in several hundred years."
- Lore Bible: "The glow color is unique to each individual." By that rule nobody has had it before.
- Possible fix: Tantris is an old elf, so "a few years" could be an elf's dry understatement for centuries. If that's the intent, the Summary's open thread should say so instead of hunting for someone from "a few years ago."

**3. "Elemental" vs. "Magical."** — Medium
- Summary (S115, S253) and Brannoch on the page call the outward discipline *Elemental*: "Fire, water, wind, stone, light."
- Lore Bible calls it *Magical*: "outward; shapes the environment. Elemental magic, arcane, divine, etc." Elemental is one branch of it, not the name of the whole discipline.
- Call: rename it in the Lore Bible, or let Brannoch's first-year lecture be a simplification and say so somewhere.

**4. Magical Application has ~200 students.** — Low
- Summary: "about 200 students" (S119).
- Lore Bible: "Classrooms: Auditorium-style, seating 40–60 students per class." Brannoch's ~50 fits that rule. Venn's 200 doesn't.

**5. Valia's lab is in the "east wing."** — Medium
- Summary: "Lab in the EAST WING" (S217, S126).
- Lore Bible, Island Layout: "Magical research facilities and libraries cluster around the central towers." The East holds the Solarium and the herb gardens.
- Call: move the lab to the center, or decide that her lab was put on the far side of the island on purpose. That would fit her pity appointment.

**6. Mythrandis Guards travel far from the island.** — Low
- Summary: Holt and Pell escort Valia across the world to Helvanthor (S12, S25). Guards in Mythrandis blue stand "on every corner" of Aethres (S81).
- Lore Bible: The Guard is "stationed at the island's edges and the bridge" and escorts students and faculty "when they travel to Aethres."
- Call: widen the Guard's remit in the Lore Bible to cover faculty escorts abroad and a presence in Aethres.

**7. Auriel's height, hair length, eye color.** — Medium
- Summary: "short," hair "loose to the hips," "amber eyes" (S121, S240).
- Auriel sheet: "Medium height," hair "to small of back," "Golden eyes."
- The page agrees with the Summary (M1202: "She was short," hair "to the curve of her hips"; M1679: "past her hips").

**8. Rinnie gives up her ribbon.** — Medium
- Summary: Gwenith holds Rinnie's blue ribbon at the bridge (S105). "Gave (or lent) her BLUE RIBBON to Gwenith" (S210).
- Rinnie sheet: "frayed ribbon — gift from Dusty; never without it."
- The beat is strong. Either the sheet changes, or the page makes clear it's a one-time message ("lent") and the ribbon goes back to her.

**9. Rinnie coughs and can't keep food down. Neither is on her sheet.** — Medium
- Summary: "she coughs at night, cold hands" (S122). "Must eat slowly or she vomits" (S205). In Scene 13 Dusty pictures her "coughing on the cot."
- Rinnie sheet symptoms: fatigue, pallor, trembling on overexertion. "Systemic weakness." No cough, no vomiting.
- The cough matters more than most details. In the manuscript the **coughing sickness** is what the guards burn streets for, and it's what killed the parents. A coughing Rinnie points readers straight at that. Decide whether that link is intended.

**10. Helvanthor: a city or a kingdom?** — Medium
- Summary: "Helvanthor: capital(-ish) city; upper city spires... the Greys" (S249).
- Brycendar sheet: Brycendar is "Crown Prince of Helvanthor," a "continent-spanning kingdom." Dusty sheet: the Greys sit in "a large metropolis."
- The page uses both. Valia says "the ones from the capital, from the Greys" (M479), and Auriel says "Helvanthor's enormous, where in Helvanthor?" (M1689).
- Neither the Summary nor any sheet says the obvious thing: **Dusty is Brycendar's subject.** The "crown's own" guards who burned his parents' street (M227) serve Brycendar's father. Brycendar is a first year crossing the same bridge as Dusty. Worth recording in both sheets and the Summary, and worth deciding whether the capital city is also named Helvanthor.

**11. Helvanthor is at war with the North.** — Medium
- Summary: "Helvanthor has been at war with THE NORTH since he was small" (S73, S249).
- Aivriel sheet: queen of the Northern Kingdom, family "killed in war," crowned at 7. Brycendar sheet: Helvanthor's strength is "military dominance."
- Neither sheet names the other's kingdom as the enemy. If the war is Helvanthor against the North, then two of the leads are the crowned heads of nations at war. That's big enough to need a decision and an entry on both sheets.

**12. Potions aren't mana. The Lore Bible files them under healing mana.** — Low
- Summary: "potions are brewed from PLANTS, not mana" (S163, S246).
- Lore Bible, Healing: "Includes herbalism, gardening, potion-making, poultices (these are minor areas)."
- These can both be true: potion-making is a healer's craft that runs on plants. The Lore Bible line should say so.

**13. The enrollment deal and its stakes.** — Medium
- Dusty sheet: "The deal: Dusty enrolls, Rinnie gets housed in Aethres under academy healer care. Expulsion... means Rinnie loses that care." This is his **Major Fear**. The Lore Bible says the same.

- As written, Rinnie's care hangs on Valia and the Maids, not on Dusty's grades or conduct. His biggest fear has nothing on the page to hold it up yet. The Summary's own rework note for the Rariel scene (S301) asks for this. It needs a line from Valia or Antonaez.

**14. Who looks after Valia's lab.** — Low
- Valia sheet: "Maid Corp assigns one maid per day."
- Summary: two specific Maids, Gwenith and Maedelyn, "take turns in her lab" (S92, S227).
- Compatible if the two rotate days. Update the sheet to name them.

**15. Who isolates Auriel.** — Low
- Summary, open thread: "the Church, via Rariel" (S270).
- Auriel sheet: "Church leverages Rariel **and Antonaez** to isolate her." The Summary leaves out the Headmaster.

**16. "Just over three hundred" non-nobles.** — Low
- Summary: 4–5k students, "just over 300 non-noble" (S75).
- Lore Bible: the academy trains "noble/merchant children." The Hundred Isles is ruled by merchant families with no hereditary nobility. If merchant children count as non-noble, 300 looks low. Decide whether Valia means commoners rather than non-nobles.

**17. A second gold glow.** — Low
- Summary: the bull-necked noble has a "gold glow" (S151).
- Brycendar sheet: "Golden Glow." Lore Bible: glow color is unique to each person. Auriel's aura is also yellow and gold. Watch this so Brycendar's color stays his own. The noble could be amber or brass.

## Part 3 — Conflicts between the reference files themselves

These decide which side of a discrepancy is "right," so they're worth settling first.

1. **Rinnie vs. Dusty sheets:** the parents' deaths and the slum's name. Already flagged in the Summary.
2. **Antonaez sheet "two thousand students"** vs. the Lore Bible's ~5,000. Already flagged in the Summary.
3. **Valia sheet, "Child Valia found the notes."** The same sheet has her father dying at her graduation, at age 45. The Lore Bible makes elves biologically mature at 20. She was an elven adolescent, not a child.
4. **Glow on the body.** Lore Bible: "Only the hands — the body does not glow." Auriel sheet: "When active, her whole body glows yellow and golden." Divine aura may be the exception. If so, say so in the Lore Bible.
5. **Who can summon.** Lore Bible: Summoning is divine magic, "Granted by the gods to very few individuals. Only women." The Antonaez and Valia sheets: Antonaez's summoning experiment pulled a demonic god into the world. Decide whether his method was non-divine (research, ritual, the catacombs) and note it.
6. **The Headmaster's pronoun.** The Lore Bible uses "they," and the Antonaez sheet and the page use "he." Probably just the Lore Bible being generic. Align it.
7. **Reserve scale.** The Lore Bible runs wells → ponds → lakes → oceans. Brycendar's sheet uses "Seas of Mana" / "Sea-level," and Dusty's uses "a shallow puddle." Both sit outside the scale. Add puddle and sea to the Lore Bible, or reword the sheets.
8. **Evangelise sheet, "Attending Athres."** That's a misspelling of Aethres, and she's a student, so it should probably read Mythrandis.
9. **Aelith sheet, eyes.** "pale grey-green eyes" and "Pale green eyes" sit back to back in the same line.
10. **Antonaez, "No living person remembers before him"** (~600 years). The Lore Bible has Orders "still guided by living elven founders" with networks "stretching back centuries," and spirits older than two millennia. Tantris is an elf whose years "run past counting." Narrow the line to "no living human," or accept that some elves remember.

## Part 4 — On the page but missing from the Lore Bible

These aren't conflicts. They're established facts with no home in the reference files yet, and future pages will need to match them.

- **Way Stations:** run by the Church. The crystals are grown from a thumbnail seed over decades, ring like a bell when they fire, wear down with use until they crack, and cost a fortune. Travel shifts the time of day. Valia has used them 30–40 times.
- **Places:** Calvenne. The Duke's unnamed honey-brick town, five days' ride from Aethres. Helvanthor's upper city, lower market, the Greys (a slum built over a buried river), the Devout Quarter, and the cathedral.
- **Helvanthor's guards** burn out streets struck by the coughing sickness, with people still inside. Helvanthor is at war with the North.
- **The Maids' origin:** "Long ago, the Maids served a monster. When he died, we chose to serve the academy instead."
- **Tea mechanics:** tell any Maid an hour ahead where you'll host and how many are coming, and it will be waiting. A single bell ends tea at 4:30. A Maid stands in every courtyard. Nobody sees the service carried in.
- **Admissions:** about 300 non-nobles, admitted through talent, noble sponsorship, or random clergy picks from villages. Returning students cross first and get a day to settle, and first years cross the next day. First years are sorted into groups of 30 under a Maid. Schedules are slipped under doors. Each room door has a brass name plate.
- **Alignment testing:** three roped pens (Stroud, Venn, Tantris). This year's split came out 20 healing, 60 elemental, 120 martial.
- **Potions:** each potion does one thing. A restorative fills about a third of your reserve, gives a headache, and three in a day means vomiting. Plants named so far: starwick, redthorn, coughwort. Iron ruins preparations.
- **Island details:** the swan courtyard down the navy passage. Moonsilk roses from the eastern beds. Mythrandis colors are blue with a white tower.
